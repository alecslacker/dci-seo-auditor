Verify that each material factual claim has enough support to identify,
verify, and interpret its source. Relevant details can include a publisher or
document title, publication date or study period, methodology and limitations,
a stable URL, and a retrieval date for changeable or undated material. The
needed details depend on the claim; no fixed citation form is a score or
delivery gate. Unverifiable claims must be removed or replaced.
